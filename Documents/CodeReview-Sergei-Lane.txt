1: Long Method
Within Player.updateInternal, the method alone ran lots of code, including the stun handling, input handling, and movement.
This was refactored to split off into an input handling method, which then calls to a move method.
The stun may further be split into it's own method later.

2: Parent gets from child
Within MobileEntity, the parent abstracts a getAttackRange method which is continuously drawn from by the abstracted part.
Instead, this was changed such that the abstracted counterpart sets the variable for the attack range upon creation such that
no indirection is needed to obtain this constant data.

3: Long Method
Within MobileEntity, the AStar path construction within updateInternal was a very long process.
This was refactored into multiple methods outside of updateInternal.
